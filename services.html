Services

Simulation expertise, delivered your way.

Whether you need a bespoke tool, a set of simulations run for you, or an expert opinion, we help you get reliable, mechanism-informed answers to your drug delivery questions.

Custom software development

We design and build simulation software tailored to your delivery system — from focused calculation tools to complete graphical platforms your team can use every day.

Typical work

  • Bespoke models for your nanoparticle, tissue or device
  • Graphical interfaces and workflow automation
  • Integration with lab, imaging and existing data pipelines
  • Deployment, documentation and user training

You receive

  • Working, tested software
  • Source code or licensed builds, as agreed
  • Documentation and example cases
  • Maintenance and update options

Simulation services

No time to learn a new tool? Send us your problem. We set up the model, run the cases, analyse the results and explain what they mean for your formulation.

Typical studies

  • Penetration and distribution in spheroids, organoids and tissue
  • Barrier crossing and transport-pathway identification
  • Drug release kinetics and mass transfer
  • Parametric studies and formulation optimisation

You receive

  • A clear technical report
  • Publication-quality figures and animations
  • Raw and processed simulation data
  • A debrief meeting with our team

Scientific advisory

Practical, model-guided advice for drug delivery projects — helping you interpret mechanisms, prioritise experiments and design better formulations.

We help with

  • Interpreting delivery outcomes mechanistically
  • Experimental design and parameter selection
  • Reducing trial-and-error and unnecessary animal studies
  • In silico components of grants and research projects

Formats

  • One-off consultations
  • Project-based advisory
  • Ongoing advisory arrangements

Computational consulting

Expert support for teams building their own modelling capability, from method selection through verification to high-performance computing.

Expertise

  • Particle-scale and multiscale transport modelling
  • Computational fluid dynamics and coupled fluid–particle simulation
  • Model calibration, validation and uncertainty quantification
  • Physics-informed machine learning and surrogate models

Engagements

  • Technical reviews of models and code
  • Method development
  • Code optimisation and HPC
  • Workshops and training
How we work

A simple, transparent process.

  1. Step 1

    Discuss

    A free initial conversation to understand your question, data and timeline.

  2. Step 2

    Scope

    A clear proposal with objectives, deliverables, timeline and a fixed or staged quote.

  3. Step 3

    Model & simulate

    We build, calibrate and run the models, with regular check-ins along the way.

  4. Step 4

    Deliver & support

    Results, report and handover — plus follow-up support when you need it.
